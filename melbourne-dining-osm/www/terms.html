Terms and privacy

This desktop prototype supports a University of Melbourne tourism visualisation project. Restaurant information may be missing or outdated. Distances are straight-line estimates; this service does not provide directions or bookings. Opening status and hours are supplied by Google and should be confirmed with the restaurant.

Google Maps

Use of Google Maps features is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When configured, the browser loads Google Places UI Kit. Clicking Search sends the selected landmark coordinates, radius and restaurant type directly to Google. Selecting a restaurant can request its details. The app does not request your live location. Current restaurant results stay in browser session memory, are removed when expired, and are not intentionally saved to files, persistent browser storage or analytics.

OpenStreetMap

The map uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap contributors' data. Your browser requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap and the Leaflet library from unpkg. These providers receive normal connection metadata such as your IP address and requested resources. See the OSMF privacy policy. No bulk downloading or offline tile prefetching is performed.

Landmark data

Landmarks are derived from City of Melbourne open data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Fields have been normalized and stable identifiers generated. City of Melbourne does not endorse this prototype.

Prototype status

No account, favourites or reservation service is provided. Before public deployment the group must add the actual operator's contact details and review hosting logs, retention settings, API restrictions and data attribution.
